# Restaurant Startup (외식업 창업)

Compressed reference for restaurant business startups: location analysis, menu engineering, unit economics, staffing models, licensing, and go/no-go decision frameworks for food service ventures in Korea and similar markets.

## Location Analysis (상권분석)

### Core Location Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Benchmarks |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **유동인구 (Foot Traffic)** | Peak-hour pedestrians per 15 min x 4 x operating hours | Lunch spot: 200+/hr; Dinner spot: 100+/hr; Bar zone: 150+/hr after 6pm |
| **배후수요 (Demand Base)** | Office workers + residents within 500m radius | Office: 3,000+ workers; Residential: 5,000+ households |
| **임대료 비율 (Rent Ratio)** | Monthly Rent / Expected Monthly Revenue x 100 | <10% excellent; 10-15% healthy; 15-20% tight; >20% dangerous |
| **권리금 회수기간 (Key Money Payback)** | Key Money / Monthly Net Profit | <12mo good; 12-24mo acceptable; >24mo risky |
| **경쟁강도 (Competition Density)** | Same-category stores within 300m | 0-2 low; 3-5 moderate; >5 saturated |

### Location Scoring Framework

Evaluate on five dimensions (each 1-5 scale, total 25):

1. **접근성 (Accessibility)** -- Subway distance, parking, main road visibility
2. **유동인구 (Traffic Volume)** -- Peak and off-peak flow, target demographic match
3. **배후수요 (Demand Pool)** -- Office density, residential density, student population
4. **경쟁환경 (Competitive Landscape)** -- Same category saturation, complementary businesses
5. **성장성 (Growth Potential)** -- New developments, infrastructure plans, demographic trends

**Score interpretation:** 20-25 prime; 15-19 viable; 10-14 risky; <10 avoid.

### Location Red Flags

- Rent increase clause >5% annually without revenue share option
- Landlord history of not renewing leases at successful locations
- Upcoming construction blocking access for 6+ months
- Single anchor tenant (one large office) providing >60% of area traffic
- Zoning changes in progress that restrict food service

## Menu Engineering (메뉴 설계)

### Menu Matrix (BCG-Style)

| Category | Popularity | Profit Margin | Strategy |
|----------|-----------|---------------|----------|
| **스타 (Star)** | High | High | Maintain, feature prominently |
| **효자 (Cash Cow)** | High | Low | Raise price carefully or reduce cost |
| **퍼즐 (Puzzle)** | Low | High | Promote, reposition, rename |
| **개 (Dog)** | Low | Low | Remove or replace |

### Pricing Formulas

| Method | Formula | When to Use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **원가기반 (Cost-Plus)** | Food Cost x Markup (3-4x) | Baseline pricing, commodity items |
| **경쟁기반 (Market)** | Competitor avg +/- positioning | Established categories (김치찌개, 삼겹살) |
| **가치기반 (Value)** | Willingness-to-pay from testing | Unique items, signature dishes |

### Food Cost Targets by Category

| Restaurant Type | Target Food Cost % | Acceptable Range |
|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 한식 (Korean) | 30-35% | 28-38% |
| 중식 (Chinese) | 28-32% | 25-35% |
| 일식 (Japanese) | 32-38% | 30-42% |
| 양식 (Western) | 28-33% | 25-36% |
| 카페 (Cafe) | 15-25% | 12-30% |
| 치킨/피자 (Chicken/Pizza) | 35-40% | 32-45% |
| 배달전문 (Delivery-only) | 30-35% | 28-38% |

### Menu Size Guidelines

- 전문점 (Specialty): 8-15 items optimal. Focus wins.
- 한식당 (Korean restaurant): 15-25 items. More than 30 = kitchen chaos.
- 카페 (Cafe): 20-30 drinks + 10-15 food items.
- Rule: If any item sells <5% of total orders, evaluate for removal monthly.

## Unit Economics (손익 구조)

### Restaurant P&L Structure

| Category | % of Revenue | Benchmark |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **식재료비 (Food Cost / COGS)** | 28-35% | >40% is a crisis |
| **인건비 (Labor)** | 25-30% | Including owner salary |
| **임대료 (Rent)** | 10-15% | >20% unsustainable |
| **공과금/기타 (Utilities/Other)** | 5-8% | Gas, electric, water, waste |
| **마케팅 (Marketing)** | 3-5% | Higher in first 6 months |
| **감가상각 (Depreciation)** | 3-5% | Interior + equipment |
| **영업이익 (Operating Profit)** | 10-20% | <10% fragile; >20% excellent |

### Break-Even Analysis

| Metric | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| **손익분기 매출 (BEP Revenue)** | Fixed Costs / (1 - Variable Cost %) |
| **손익분기 객수 (BEP Covers)** | BEP Revenue / Average Ticket |
| **손익분기 일수 (BEP Days)** | BEP Monthly Revenue / (Daily Revenue) |
| **투자회수기간 (ROI Period)** | Total Investment / Monthly Net Profit |

### Investment Structure

| Item | Typical Range (Korean market, 20-30평) |
|------|----------------------------------------|
| **보증금 (Deposit)** | 3,000-8,000만원 |
| **권리금 (Key Money)** | 2,000-15,000만원 (location-dependent) |
| **인테리어 (Interior)** | 3,000-8,000만원 (평당 150-300만원) |
| **장비/집기 (Equipment)** | 2,000-5,000만원 |
| **초기재료/기타 (Initial Stock)** | 500-1,000만원 |
| **운전자금 (Working Capital)** | 3개월 고정비 (최소) |
| **총 투자 (Total)** | 1.5-4억원 typical for small restaurant |

**Critical rule:** Always reserve 3 months of fixed costs as working capital. Restaurants that open without runway fail in the first 6 months.

### Daily Revenue Targets

| Metric | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| **목표 일매출** | Monthly BEP / Operating Days |
| **객단가 목표** | Target Daily Revenue / Target Daily Covers |
| **좌석회전율** | Daily Covers / Total Seats |

**Seat turnover benchmarks:** Lunch 1.5-2.5x; Dinner 1.0-1.5x; Cafe 3-5x; Bar 1.5-2.0x.

## Staffing & Operations (인력/운영)

### Staffing Ratios

| Restaurant Type | Seats per Staff | Kitchen:Hall Ratio |
|----------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 한식/중식 (Korean/Chinese) | 8-12 seats/person | 1:1 or kitchen-heavy |
| 일식 (Japanese) | 6-10 seats/person | Kitchen-heavy (2:1) |
| 카페 (Cafe) | 15-20 seats/person | All-rounder model |
| 배달전문 (Delivery) | N/A | Kitchen-only + packing |

### Labor Cost Control

- 인건비 비율 목표: 25-30% of revenue (including owner labor value)
- 최저임금 기준 시급 x 근무시간 x 직원수 = minimum monthly labor cost
- Peak/off-peak staffing: use part-time (알바) for peaks, reduce to skeleton off-peak
- Owner labor trap: if removing your own labor makes the business unprofitable, the business model is broken

### Operational Checklists

**Daily:**
- Open: ingredient check, prep list, equipment check, cleanliness
- Close: inventory count (high-value items), sales reconciliation, prep for tomorrow

**Weekly:**
- Full inventory count
- Food cost % calculation (purchases / revenue)
- Staff schedule optimization based on last week's traffic patterns
- Menu item sales mix analysis

**Monthly:**
- Full P&L review
- Menu engineering matrix update
- Supplier price comparison
- Customer feedback synthesis

## Licensing & Compliance (인허가)

### Required Permits (Korean Market)

| Permit | Issuing Authority | Timeline |
|--------|------------------|----------|
| **영업신고 (Business Registration)** | 관할 세무서 | 1-2 days |
| **식품위생교육 (Food Hygiene Training)** | 한국외식산업협회 | 1 day course |
| **영업허가/신고 (F&B License)** | 관할 구청 보건소 | 7-14 days |
| **소방안전점검 (Fire Safety)** | 소방서 | Required if >66㎡ |
| **위생등급 (Hygiene Grade)** | 식약처 | Optional but builds trust |

### Common Compliance Failures

- 배기/환기 (Ventilation) not meeting standards for cooking type
- 화장실 (Restroom) requirements not met for seating capacity
- 간판 (Signage) permit missing or oversized
- 음식물쓰레기 (Food waste) disposal contract not arranged
- 주류면허 (Liquor license) missing for alcohol service

## Go/No-Go Decision Framework

### Five Gates (모든 게이트 통과 필요)

| Gate | Pass Criteria | Fail = Stop |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| **1. 시장검증 (Market)** | Target customers exist in sufficient numbers within trade area | No demand pool = no business |
| **2. 단위경제 (Unit Economics)** | BEP achievable within 3 months, ROI <24 months | Never breaks even = money pit |
| **3. 차별화 (Differentiation)** | Clear reason customers choose you over 3+ alternatives | "Tastier" without proof is not differentiation |
| **4. 운영역량 (Operational Capability)** | Team can execute menu at target quality and speed consistently | Great concept with poor execution = failure |
| **5. 재무여력 (Financial Cushion)** | Total investment + 6 months runway available | Opening with zero buffer = gambling |

### Risk Scoring

For each risk factor, score 1 (low) to 5 (high impact x probability):

- **임대 리스크:** Lease term, renewal terms, rent escalation
- **경쟁 리스크:** New entrants, delivery platform competition
- **인력 리스크:** Chef dependency, hiring difficulty in area
- **식재료 리스크:** Supply chain fragility, seasonal price volatility
- **규제 리스크:** Zoning changes, health regulations, minimum wage increases

**Total risk score:** <10 manageable; 10-15 proceed with mitigation plan; >15 reconsider entirely.

## Delivery & Platform Strategy (배달/플랫폼)

### Platform Economics

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **수수료 후 마진 (Post-Commission Margin)** | (Order Revenue - Commission - Food Cost - Packaging) / Order Revenue | >20% |
| **배달 비중 (Delivery Mix)** | Delivery Revenue / Total Revenue x 100 | 30-50% for hybrid; 100% ghost kitchen |
| **주문당 수익 (Profit per Order)** | Revenue - Commission - COGS - Packaging - Delivery subsidy | Must be positive |

### Platform Commission Ranges (Korean Market)

| Platform | Commission Range | Note |
|----------|-----------------|------|
| 배달의민족 | 6.8-12.5% + 광고비 | Ultra Call vs. Open List |
| 쿠팡이츠 | 9.8-15% | Includes delivery |
| 요기요 | 12.5% + listing fees | Super List premium |

### Delivery Menu Pricing Rule

Delivery price = Dine-in price + (Commission % x Price) + Packaging cost. Or: raise delivery menu 10-15% above dine-in. Customers accept this if value is clear.

## Trend & Concept Validation (트렌드/컨셉 검증)

### Concept Validation Checklist

Before committing capital:

1. **검색량 분석 (Search Volume):** Naver/Google trends for category. Rising? Stable? Declining?
2. **경쟁자 매출 추정:** Delivery app review counts, 카드매출 data (from commercial data services)
3. **소규모 테스트:** Pop-up, 공유주방 (shared kitchen), weekend market stall
4. **타겟 인터뷰:** 10+ potential customers, willingness to pay, frequency expectation
5. **벤치마크 방문:** Visit 5+ similar concepts, assess strengths/weaknesses firsthand

### Trend Lifecycle Assessment

| Stage | Signal | Strategy |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **도입기 (Introduction)** | Media buzz, few players, high margins | First-mover advantage, premium pricing |
| **성장기 (Growth)** | New entrants weekly, franchises forming | Differentiate hard or scale fast |
| **성숙기 (Maturity)** | Saturation, price wars, closures starting | Only enter with clear cost or differentiation edge |
| **쇠퇴기 (Decline)** | Steady closures, declining search volume | Do not enter. Period. |

## Interaction Rules (Coaching Mode)

When coaching is active for restaurant startup topics, use these rules.

| Trigger | Action | Stop When |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| User presents location without foot traffic data | Push: "Have you counted actual foot traffic at peak hours? Estimates from feelings are the #1 cause of location failure." | User provides measured or sourced data |
| Investment plan lacks working capital | Challenge: "Where is your 3-month runway? Opening a restaurant with zero buffer is gambling, not entrepreneurship." | User adds buffer or explains alternative |
| Menu has 30+ items planned for a new restaurant | Push: "Complexity kills new restaurants. Which 10 items can you execute perfectly every single day?" | User narrows focus |
| Food cost % not calculated | Challenge: "Do you know your actual food cost per dish? Guessing margins is how restaurants bleed out slowly." | User provides per-item costing |
| Concept based solely on personal taste | Push: "You like it. Will 100+ strangers pay for it weekly? What's your evidence beyond personal preference?" | User names external validation |
| User has answered 2 follow-ups on the same point | Stop pushing. Summarize what's still weak and give best-guess output. | -- |
